## 1.5 MeshMesh & Maestro — the strategic frame

**Maestro** is Salesforce's new AI-native, agent-driven initiative designed to make using and implementing *any* Salesforce product simple. Its vision:

> **The Maestro Vision — "We make the complex, Salesforce simple."**

**MeshMesh is part of Maestro — and the engine proving it works, today.** Maestro sets the direction; MeshMesh is the running proof, with real users delivering real outcomes in real orgs right now.

**The Maestro value motion:** **Diagnose → Fix → Innovate** — assess the org and surface what's broken or missing, clear the backlog, then move to net-new capability.

**Writer's rule.** At the strategy or executive level, name **Maestro** as the initiative and MeshMesh as the **engine** (*"Maestro is the vision. MeshMesh is the engine proving it works, today."*). Keep the two motions distinct: **Diagnose → Fix → Innovate** is the Maestro value arc; **Define → Approve → Execute** (§6) is how a single MeshMesh task runs.

## 3. What MeshMesh is (and is not)

**Is:**
- A **Salesforce-first Headless 360 orchestration platform** that co-creates a detailed plan with you, then executes the full build autonomously.
- A **closed-loop agentic intelligence platform** — structured knowledge across 40+ Salesforce products plus a compounding three-context model.
- **Interface-agnostic**: the same intelligence runs from MeshMesh Studio, Slack, a Lightning app, an IDE (Cursor / Claude), or any MCP client.

**Is not (use these to draw contrast, never to disparage):**
- **Not an IDE extension or a dev tool.** Cursor, Claude Code, and Copilot know code, not Salesforce.
- **Not "prompt-to-generate."** MeshMesh builds a *plan*, not a vibe — structure instead of loose prompting.
- **Not an LLM wrapper.** It is a structured intelligence asset representing thousands of agent-hours of validated processing.
- **Not a replacement for Agentforce.** *MeshMesh doesn't replace Agentforce — it builds Agentforce… and beyond.* Agentforce is what your customers use; MeshMesh builds what your customers use.

## 6. The mental model (how to explain it in 15 seconds)

**Explicit intent in. Enterprise-grade execution out.**

1. **Define outcomes** — describe what you need in Salesforce, in plain language.
2. **Approve the plan** — MeshMesh builds a plan from your context, org metadata, and Salesforce best practices; you approve it.
3. **Execute** — end-to-end delivery: configs, data, automations, agents — production-ready, with verified outcomes.

Humans stay at the **bookends** (planning and validation); the hours in between come back to you.

## 8. Boundaries & disclaimers (always honor)

- **Human-in-the-loop is not optional messaging.** Never imply MeshMesh makes autonomous changes to production without explicit human approval. Planning and execution are strictly separated by an approval gate.
- **Poor-fit segments** (do not target in copy): GovTech / highly regulated, and software-development companies.
- **Legal usage tiers exist.** SE demos/POCs without touching customer orgs are approved; other uses depend on the governing agreement. Don't make blanket "use it anywhere" claims.
- **Confidential figures.** The per-use-case dollar and hour tables in the Product & Technology Overview are marked *Internal Only* — keep them out of public-facing copy unless cleared.
